## My Favorite Movie:

If you will ask me about my favorite movie, a lot of them comes to mind.
From genres of Sci-fi, Action, Suspense, Drama, and monsters.
I have my choices.

But, if I need to choose only one movie, I'll pick **Pacific Rim.**
I've watched that movie a couple of times and I never get tired of it.
Big monsters and big robots fighting together are my cup of tea, it never bores me.
Saving the world from extra terrestrial beings made the whole word help each other to delay the apocalypse.
The persistence of the human race to fight for what is worth can be seen in this movie.
As Rally said, **""There are things you can't fight, acts of God. You see a hurricane coming, you have to get out of the way. But when you're in a Jaeger, suddenly, you can fight the hurricane. You can win."**
